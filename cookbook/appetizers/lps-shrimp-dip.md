If this dip was out, we were having some peel-and-eat shrimp -- yum!

## Ingredients

- [x] 1 tablespoon ketchup
- [x] 2 tablespoons chili sauce
- [x] 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- [x] 1 tablespoon ground horseradish
- [x] 1 tablespoon Miracle Whip
- [x] &frac12; teaspoon Worcestershire sauce

## Instructions

- Mix ingredients in a shallow bowl.
- Dip cooked and peeled shrimp into mixture and taste several times, making minor adjustments with the listed ingredients until observers catch on to the fact that you are eating too many
shrimp.
