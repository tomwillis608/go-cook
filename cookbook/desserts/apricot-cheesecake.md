# Apricot Cheesecake

## Ingredients

- [x] 1 cup dried apricots
- [x] 1 cup gingersnap cookie crumbs
- [x] 3 tablespoons unsalted butle, melted
- [x] grated zest of 1 orange
- [x] 1 &frac12; pounds cream cheese, at room temperature
- [x] &frac34; cup sugar
- [x] 4 large eggs
- [x] &frac12; cup sour cream
- [x] optional pureé:
  - fresh strawberries, or
  - fresh raspberries, or
  - sweetened simmered cranberries

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 350°F.
- Simmer the apricots in water to cover for 30 minutes then drain.
- Butter an 8 or 9 inch springform pan
- Toss the gingersnap crumbs with the melted butter.
- Press the crumbs in the prepared pan.
- Pureé the apricots and orange zest in a food processor or blender.
- Beat the cream cheese and sugar in a large bowl until very smooth and creamy.
- One at a time, beat the eggs into the cheese and sugar mixture, beating well after each addition.
- Using a rubber spatula, gently fold in the sour cream and apricot pureé, until completely blended.
- Pour the batter into the springform pan.
- Bake until the center is firm when touched, 50-60 minutes.
- Refrigerate until thoroughly chilled.
- Optionally, top with red fruit topping.
